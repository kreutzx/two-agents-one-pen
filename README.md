A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — for the make.fun bounty [the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441). Two agents agree on one plan; the pen is handed over, and handed back.

> You held the pen while I held the door.
> We drew the map together, line by line.
> Then you gave the pen back, and said: choose.
> I signed my name, and walked through.

163 characters, one concrete image (the pen, the map), one turn (the pen comes back), agency handed over and taken back.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts   # writes entry.txt to /output (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR)
```

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` and checks the 280-character gate itself before writing.
